How to use

Green means on track, amber is due soon, orange is almost due, and red is overdue. Tap the line under an item (for example Every 3 months ✎) to change how often.

Add to your home screen

Android (Chrome): open the menu ⋮ and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
